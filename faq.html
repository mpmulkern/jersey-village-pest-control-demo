Common Questions

How often should you get pest control in Houston?

Most Houston-area homes do best on a quarterly recurring plan (every 90 days). Homes with heavier pest pressure, near standing water or wooded lots, often benefit from monthly service during peak season, April through October.

Are pest control treatments safe for pets and kids?

Yes. We use EPA-registered products applied by a licensed technician with targeted placement to limit indoor exposure, and we provide clear re-entry guidance so you know when it's safe for children and pets to return to treated areas.

What pests are most common in Jersey Village and Houston?

Mosquitoes, subterranean termites, cockroaches (German and American), ants, and rodents (mice and rats) are the most common pests in the Jersey Village and greater Houston area, driven by the region's humidity and warm climate.

Do you offer one-time treatments or only recurring plans?

Both are available. One-time treatment suits an isolated, already-identified issue. For ongoing prevention, most homeowners choose a quarterly or monthly recurring plan, which is more cost-effective over a year and prevents pests from re-establishing.

How long does a typical pest control visit take?

A standard recurring service visit typically takes 20 to 40 minutes, covering interior and exterior treatment. A termite inspection can take longer, typically 30 to 60 minutes depending on the size and construction of the home.

Do I need to leave my home during treatment?

Usually not for a standard recurring visit; targeted application allows most homeowners to remain home. We provide specific re-entry guidance for any room or area treated directly.

What time of year is pest activity worst in Houston?

Mosquito and general pest activity peaks April through October in the Houston metro. Termite swarming season is typically spring, and rodents often become more active indoors in fall as temperatures drop.
