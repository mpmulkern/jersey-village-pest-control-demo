Pest Control in Cypress, TX

Cypress is one of our busiest service areas outside Jersey Village itself, and the pest profile here is distinctive. Rapid residential development across Bridgeland, Fairfield, Towne Lake and Coles Crossing has put thousands of homes next to retention ponds, drainage channels and recently cleared land, which produces heavier mosquito and fire ant pressure than established neighbourhoods closer to Houston.

Why New Construction Means More Ants and Mosquitoes

When land is cleared and regraded for a new Cypress subdivision, existing fire ant colonies are disturbed and the resulting open sunny turf is close to ideal habitat for them to re-establish. New sod arrives with its own colonies. The result is that a three-year-old neighbourhood frequently has heavier fire ant pressure than a thirty-year-old one, which surprises homeowners moving out from inside the Beltway.

Retention ponds and detention basins create the same effect for mosquitoes. They are required for drainage across the Cypress Creek watershed, and they hold shallow water through the warm months, giving mosquitoes a continuous breeding source within flight range of surrounding homes. Yard-level treatment matters more here than in areas without standing water nearby, because the source cannot be removed.

Seasonal Pattern in Cypress

Mosquito activity ramps up in April and typically runs through to November, with the sharpest spikes three to five days after heavy rain. Fire ant mounds appear most visibly in spring and again in early autumn, which is when broadcast baiting is most effective. Rodent activity moves indoors from late October as night temperatures drop, concentrated in attics and garages. Termite swarms are most commonly reported from March through May, usually after a warm rain.

Quarterly service is timed against this pattern rather than at fixed calendar intervals, so each visit lands ahead of the next seasonal pressure rather than after it.

Family and Pet-Safe Treatment

We will always tell you the re-entry interval for what was applied before we leave the property.
