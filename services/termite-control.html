Warning Signs to Watch For

  • Mud tubes on exterior walls, piers, or foundation
  • Hollow-sounding or damaged wood trim, framing, or flooring
  • Discarded wings near windows and doors, especially in spring
  • Small holes or blistering in drywall or painted wood surfaces

What are signs of a termite problem?
