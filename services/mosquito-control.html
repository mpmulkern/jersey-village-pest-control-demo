Mosquito Control in Jersey Village, TX

Houston's humidity and long warm season give mosquitoes months longer to breed than in most of Texas. Jersey Village Pest Control Pros treats yards with a barrier application and reduces standing-water breeding sites, so you can use your yard again without the usual Gulf Coast mosquito pressure.

Direct Answer: How Long Does Treatment Last?

A standard barrier application controls mosquito activity for about 3–4 weeks. Because Houston's mosquito season runs roughly April through November, most homeowners choose monthly in-season service rather than a single treatment.

What's Included

  • Barrier treatment to vegetation, shaded areas, and yard perimeter where adult mosquitoes rest
  • Identification and treatment of standing-water breeding sites on the property
  • Optional in-season monthly service for continuous coverage, April through October
  • One-time treatment available for an event or short-term need

Why Houston Needs More Than a One-Time Spray

Mosquitoes breed in as little as a bottle cap of standing water and complete a life cycle in under two weeks during Houston's warm months. A single treatment interrupts the current adult population but not the eggs already laid, which is why recurring in-season service consistently outperforms a one-off spray for yards with any standing water nearby (gutters, plant saucers, drainage areas).

Common Questions

How long does mosquito barrier treatment last?

A standard barrier treatment typically reduces mosquito activity for about 3 to 4 weeks, which is why in-season monthly service is recommended for continuous coverage during Houston's long mosquito season.

Is mosquito treatment safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We use EPA-registered products applied by a licensed technician with standard re-entry guidance, typically allowing children and pets back in the treated yard once the application has dried, usually within a couple of hours.
