Rodent Control in Jersey Village, TX

Mice and rats look for warmth, food, and shelter, and a Houston-area home offers plenty of all three, especially as temperatures cool in fall. Jersey Village Pest Control Pros combines trapping with entry-point exclusion, so the current problem is removed and the path they used to get in is sealed.

Direct Answer: Trapping vs. Exclusion

Trapping removes rodents already inside the home. Exclusion seals the entry points they used to get in. Lasting rodent control needs both — trapping alone without sealing entry points is why rodents tend to come back.

Our Rodent Control Process

Inspection

We identify entry points, nesting areas, and activity level around the foundation, attic, and utility penetrations.

Trapping

Removal of existing mice or rats using appropriate trap placement, checked and serviced on a regular schedule.

Exclusion & Sealing

Physical sealing of gaps and entry points around utility lines, foundation, and roofline to prevent new rodents from entering.

Signs of a Rodent Problem

  • Droppings near food storage areas or along walls and baseboards
  • Gnaw marks on wood, wiring, or stored materials
  • Scratching sounds in walls, attics, or ceilings, especially at night
  • A stale, musky odor in enclosed spaces such as attics or crawl spaces

Common Questions

What's the difference between trapping and exclusion?

Trapping removes rodents already inside the home. Exclusion seals the entry points they used to get in, such as gaps around utility lines, foundation cracks, and roofline vents, so new rodents cannot follow the same path. Lasting control needs both.

How do I know if I have a rodent problem?

Common signs include droppings near food storage or along walls, gnaw marks on wood or wiring, scratching sounds in walls or attics (especially at night), and a stale, musky odor in enclosed spaces.
