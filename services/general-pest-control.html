Recurring Pest Control Plans in Jersey Village, TX

Jersey Village Pest Control Pros' recurring plans are built for Houston's near-year-round pest pressure. Rather than a one-time spray that fades in weeks, a recurring plan keeps a protective barrier in place and interrupts breeding cycles before an infestation can re-establish.

Direct Answer: What Does a Recurring Plan Cover?

Interior and exterior treatment for roaches, ants, spiders, silverfish, crickets, and other common household pests, applied on a quarterly or monthly schedule with free re-treatment between visits if pests return before your next scheduled service.

Plan Options

Quarterly Plan

A treatment every 90 days, covering interior and exterior perimeter, entry points, and common pest harborage areas. Our most popular plan for typical Jersey Village and Houston-metro homes.

Monthly Plan

Monthly service during peak pest season (April–October), recommended for homes near wooded lots, standing water, or with a history of heavier pest activity.

One-Time Treatment

For an isolated, already-identified issue rather than ongoing prevention. Most homeowners find a recurring plan more cost-effective over a year.

What's Covered

  • Cockroaches (German, American, and Oriental)
  • Ants, including odorous house ants and carpenter ants
  • Spiders, including common house and cellar spiders
  • Silverfish, crickets, and other occasional invaders
  • Free re-treatment between scheduled visits if pests return

Family- and Pet-Safe Application

Every visit uses EPA-registered products applied by a licensed technician, with targeted placement that limits indoor exposure and clear guidance on re-entry timing for children and pets.
