Why Houston Homes Need Year-Round Pest Control

Jersey Village and the greater Houston metro sit in a humid, subtropical climate that keeps pest pressure high nearly year-round. Mosquito season runs longer here than almost anywhere else in Texas, subterranean termite colonies are active in the region's clay-heavy soil, and roaches, ants, and rodents thrive in the warm, moist conditions between storms. A one-time treatment rarely holds; the pests that come back are usually a new generation, not survivors, which is why recurring service outperforms a single spray.

Jersey Village Pest Control Pros builds every plan around this reality: quarterly or monthly visits that interrupt the pest life cycle before it re-establishes, rather than reacting after an infestation is visible.

Direct Answer: How Often Should You Get Pest Control in Houston?

Most Houston-area homes do best on a quarterly recurring plan (every 90 days), which interrupts common pest breeding cycles year-round. Homes with heavier mosquito or ant pressure, or those near standing water or wooded lots, often benefit from a monthly plan during peak season (April–October). A one-time treatment is appropriate only for an isolated, already-resolved issue.
